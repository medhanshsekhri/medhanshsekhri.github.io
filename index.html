About.

I'm a first-year engineering student at the University of Queensland (BEng(Hons) + MEng), headed for mechanical and aerospace engineering.

Most of what I know about embedded systems came from outside the classroom. In my first few weeks of university I taught myself C++ and built an ultrasonic radar scanner around an Arduino: sweep logic, servo control, and a live visualisation in Processing. The code is on GitHub.

For ENGG1100 I led a team of four building a model house that has to hold its position in rising floodwater. My job was the motor-control circuit and the firmware, and the whole build came in under its $170 budget.

Outside uni I'm a certified remote pilot (Certificate III in Aviation) through the Australian Air Force Cadets, and I've flown a Diamond DA40 at RAAF Amberley. Current project: an obstacle-avoiding drone built on the radar work.
